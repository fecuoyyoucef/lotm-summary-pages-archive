# Ch. 601–700 Summaries

Lord of the Mysteries

Sea Voyage

601Klein releases the Psychiatrist's soul from Creeping Hunger, learning about her past and contemplating Groselle's Travels. Darkwill buys a strange die from Mabel's Sundry Store and experiences odd luck.

602Darkwill discovers a note in the die instructing him to deliver it to Carnot on Oravi Island. News spreads about Gehrman Sparrow killing Mithor King, and Tracy learns of this, suspecting Gehrman Sparrow's involvement.

603Klein meets Ralph, who tries to convert him to Sea God worship. Darkwill hires Klein as a bodyguard for 1,000 pounds and a request to find a mystical item.

604Klein and Darkwill board a ship to Oravi Island. Darkwill is struck by lightning due to bad luck from the die, highlighting the dangers they face.

605Darkwill explains that the die causes bad luck based on its roll. Klein intimidates the Die of Probability using the All-Black Eye, forcing it to cooperate.

606Klein learns from Will Auceptin about the Die of Probability and how to intimidate it. He successfully scares the die, calming it for 12 hours.

607Klein uses Sea God powers to calm a storm during the voyage. Fors Wall investigates Williams Street and finds signs of digging in an abandoned chapel.

608Klein deduces that the evil spirit is using Rafter Pound to attract members of the Einhorn and Sauron families. He reports this to the Churches via the Tarot Club.

609Klein contacts Arrodes to find a mystical item that steals Beyonder powers. Arrodes reveals that Leonard Mitchell has an angel from the Zoroast family inside him.

610Klein intimidates the Die of Probability again. A powerful Beyonder appears but is driven away by the die's bad luck effect, set to 1 point.

611Klein and Darkwill arrive at Oravi Island, meet Carnot and Councilor Ricciardo. The Die of Probability is sealed using a special item. Ricciardo attempts to augment Klein's luck, but it fails, so Darkwill wins money at casinos instead.

612Emlyn White visits Leonard Mitchell and negotiates to purchase the mystical item 'Tinder' for 7,000 pounds. Klein receives Tinder above the gray fog and purifies it of the angel's influence.

613Audrey helps her dog Susie advance to Psychiatrist. Leonard Mitchell investigates the connection to Sherlock Moriarty in the Capim case. Klein deals with a cheat impersonating him at a bar.

614Klein meets Bilt Brando, joins the Adventurer Association, and buys Sonia's Golden Spring water for his Nimblewright Master potion.

615Tarot Club meeting: Klein reads Roselle's diary entries about the Abyss and a silver plate connected to gray fog. Members discuss Lovia's release and the identities of Amon and Adam.

616Continuation of Tarot Club discussions about the True Creator and Blasphemer Amon. The World communicates privately with Emlyn White about Leonard Mitchell.

617Further Tarot Club talk about King of Angels. The World obtains footage of Leonard Mitchell from Emlyn to investigate the parasite.

618Bilt Brando and Sothoth Yann discuss Gehrman Sparrow and a mission. Klein writes to Azik and Will Auceptin for advice on dealing with the parasite in Leonard.

619Klein attempts volunteer work at the Oravi Hospice Foundation but is recognized as Gehrman Sparrow. He asks Bilt for an Artisan to create a mystical item from a murloc bladder.

620Bilt hires Klein as a stand-in for Admiral Amyrius Rieveldt for 1,000 pounds and Artisan services, requiring Klein to impersonate the demigod for a secret mission.

621Bilt Brando hires Gehrman Sparrow to act as Admiral Amyrius Rieveldt for a stand-in mission, providing detailed information and an invitation to a banquet at the governor-general's office.

622Gehrman Sparrow meets Admiral Amyrius Rieveldt, who reveals he knows Sparrow's identity and makes him sign a contract, giving him the Ninth Law charm for the mission.

623Klein, disguised as Amyrius, attends the banquet, interacts with Aston Rieveldt and Secretary Luan, and returns to the villa where he avoids intimacy with Cynthia by claiming work.

624Klein resists Cynthia's advances and later receives a dream warning from a mysterious figure representing the Twilight Hermit Order, telling him not to interfere with Aston's plans.

625Klein reflects on the warning and continues acting as Amyrius, inspecting the naval base and handling reports while maintaining his disguise.

626Aston Rieveldt is relieved of his post as governor-general, and Klein, as Amyrius, decides not to help him, citing family values and the need to protect the Rieveldt family.

627Cynthia, influenced by the Proliferation Necklace, mutates into a tree-like monster, magnifying desires in the villa; Klein uses the Ninth Law charm to isolate the external influence and kills her with Light of Holiness.

628Klein investigates the Naturism Sect, captures Flight Ken, learns about the Mother Tree of Desire's corruption plot, and kills Flight Ken, using his Beyonder characteristic for Creeping Hunger.

629Klein interrogates Flight Ken, confirming the Naturism Sect's goals and the timeline linking the corruption to his arrival on Oravi Island.

630Admiral Amyrius returns, and Klein explains the events of the past few days, including Cynthia's death and Aston's dismissal; Amyrius accepts the outcome and Klein leaves.

631Klein collects his reward from Bilt Brando, including the Murloc Cufflink, and leaves Oravi Island, reflecting on the mission's coincidences.

632Klein receives a reply from Azik about Parasite states, considers how to help Leonard Mitchell, and decides to focus on finding mermaids first.

633Klein does volunteer work at the Oravi Hospice Foundation to aid in digesting his potion, while Audrey Hall receives a mission from the Psychology Alchemists to acquire a notebook.

634Klein travels to Nas in the Gargas Archipelago to meet Admiral of Stars Cattleya, encountering protests and pirate activity along the way.

635Klein contacts Cattleya via a crystal ball at the Hot Whale Dance store, confirming their meeting and departure time for the eastern Sonia Sea voyage.

636Klein visits the Lærdal Bar in Nas, hears about an underwater ruin, and encounters Slaughter Kircheis, who later warns him not to interfere in Adventurer Association matters.

637Klein boards the Future, Admiral of Stars' flagship, meeting crew members like Frank Lee and Heath Doyle, and sets sail for the eastern Sonia Sea.

638Klein interacts with the Future's crew, learns about their abilities, and agrees to sell a Pugilist Beyonder characteristic to Cattleya to reduce his travel fee.

639The Future slows down to investigate an underwater ruin near the Gargas Archipelago, discovered by adventurer Gareth, with Nina leading the exploration.

640Nina returns from exploring the ruin, reporting a small but deep sea well, and Cattleya informs Klein that they will soon take a secret route to reach the mermaid waters faster.

641Nina explores an underwater ruin with a small well mouth and brings back samples; Cattleya decides to analyze them before exploring. Audrey Hall visits collector Michele Deuth and acquires a knight's notebook by donating to a relic foundation.

642Audrey successfully obtains the notebook and helmet from Deuth by proposing a relic foundation donation. She suspects the notebook is related to a mind dragon and decides to use magic mirror divination with Mr. Fool's help.

643Audrey performs magic mirror divination with Mr. Fool's assistance, seeing a vision of a dragon-worshiping village and the grave of the knight Lindelira. Klein on the Future encounters Frank Lee and observes a mysterious pair of eyes watching the deck.

644The Future encounters the Death Announcer, flagship of the King of Immortality Agalito, who attacks with terrifying laughter that causes sailors pain; Cattleya uses starlight powers to escape.

645After the attack, Frank Lee and Klein calm the affected sailors; the Future navigates storms and returns to safe routes. Klein warns Cattleya about Heath Doyle's susceptibility to dangerous voices.

646Fors Wall meets her teacher Dorian Gray, who gives her the Astrologer potion formula and the mystical item Leymano's Travels, warning about the traitor Lewis Wien.

647The Future enters the dangerous waters, a sea of ruins with golden midday light and floating islands. Cattleya explains the dangers, including voices that can drive demigods mad.

648Night falls, and all aboard enter a shared dream world. Klein explores a black cloister, meeting Frank, Nina, and others, while Cattleya waits on a boulder. Klein finds a mural of Ouroboros and meets Anderson Hood.

649Klein learns from Anderson about the mural and his unlucky treasure-hunting team. After waking, Cattleya confirms sleeping in the day is safe, and Klein contacts Will Auceptin for advice on the waters and mermaids.

650Will Auceptin warns against exploring the dream world due to remnants of a deity's dreams and advises on finding mermaids. The Future docks on an island to avoid a storm, where they encounter Anderson Hood in reality.

651Anderson explains his bad luck from seeing the Ouroboros mural and joins the Future as a passenger. Klein uses divination to test him and agrees to his presence for his knowledge of mermaid locations.

652Anderson reveals mermaid clues ahead. The Future continues, but Frank Lee's experiments cause the ship to produce milk and mutate due to remnant auras of Mother Earth.

653Cattleya uses starlight powers to burn away the mutations, but a pirate dies from opening a mutated watermelon. A colossal monster made of corpses attacks the Future.

654In the dream world, Klein finds himself in a prison cell and meets Leomaster, the good side of the Saint of Darkness. He battles a mirror clone and the Saint of Darkness, using the Sea God Scepter before waking.

655Klein re-enters the dream in a library, studying mysticism books. After waking, he analyzes the dream, realizing Leomaster and the Saint of Darkness are split personalities. Anderson mentions someone opening a door in the dream hall.

656Frank Lee's mutated organisms cause the Future to spew milk and grow watermelons on pirates' heads. Cattleya purges the mutations with fire, but the colossal monster attacks again.

657Cattleya and Anderson battle the monster, but its pieces remain alive. Klein uses Tinder to steal its Fog of Poison. Admiral Hell Ludwell's Black Tulip appears and attacks.

658Klein flies to the Black Tulip alone, using Azik's copper whistle to distract undead creatures. He battles Ludwell, using Tinder to steal the Language of the Dead. Anderson joins the fight.

659Klein and Anderson continue fighting Ludwell, who opens a giant bronze door to escape. Klein retrieves the copper whistle but loses his Murloc Cufflink to Tinder's negative effect.

660Ludwell retreats through the bronze door with the Black Tulip. Klein returns to the Future and digests his Faceless potion amidst the crew's admiration.

661Klein returns to the Future after battling Admiral Hell, uses divination to locate his lost Murloc Cufflink, and enters a dream library where he finds Roselle's notebook.

662Klein meets Queen Mystic in the dream world, who identifies him as Hero Bandit Black Emperor and offers to exchange answers for help with Roselle's diary.

663Klein reflects on Queen Mystic's knowledge and questions Cattleya about her motives, realizing she used him to signal Queen Mystic.

664Klein prepares and consumes the Nimblewright Master potion after finding mermaids, successfully advancing to Sequence 5 amidst their singing.

665Klein gains the Nimblewright Master ability to control Spirit Body Threads and returns to the Future, while Derrick explores Afternoon Town in the Forsaken Land.

666Derrick's team enters Afternoon Town, encounters shadows and monsters, and he discovers an altar with names of Kings of Angels.

667Derrick is trapped in an alternate Afternoon Town, prays to Mr. Fool for rescue, and is pulled above the gray fog, escaping the darkness.

668Klein learns from Queen Mystic that the ravings in the waters come from the True Creator, and they discuss the dream world's secrets.

669Queen Mystic explains the connection between the waters and the Giant King's Court, and Klein confirms her identity as Bernadette Gustav.

670Derrick's team faces shadow monsters in Afternoon Town, and an ecclesiastic reveals fragments about a calamity and tempted angels.

671Frank Lee finds a fish with human fingers, hinting at Leomaster's cry for help, and Cattleya confesses her deep feelings for Queen Mystic.

672Klein and Anderson witness Leomaster's good side fleeing in the dream, and Queen Mystic intervenes, showing the method to enter the Forsaken Land.

673The Future approaches the exit of the dangerous waters, finds a sailboat with clues about the Fountain of Unaging scam, and encounters the Death Announcer.

674The Death Announcer flees, Queen Mystic reveals the Fountain of Unaging is a demonic scam, and Klein and Anderson disembark at Toscarter Island.

675Anderson nearly chokes to death due to bad luck, seeks Klein's help, and Will Auceptin reveals Bizarro Sorcerer as the Sequence 4 name for the Seer pathway.

676Klein and Anderson negotiate a deal to remove Anderson's curse, and Klein writes to Azik and Will Auceptin for advice on advancement and parasites.

677Anderson is attacked by controlled passersby on Toscarter Island, revealing a demigod Manipulator's warning, and he apologizes to resolve the situation.

678In the Tarot Club, Mr. Fool punishes Cattleya for leaking information, and Fors requests help to assassinate an Aurora Order Oracle.

679The Tarot Club members discuss requests, with Cattleya becoming more active in providing resources, and Emlyn offers bounties for Primordial Moon believers.

680The Tarot Club continues free exchange, with Klein as The World offering the Notary potion formula, and the arc concludes as they prepare for future missions.

681In the Tarot Club, Derrick Berg shares his experiences in Afternoon Town and asks about the unspeakable King of Angels, with Klein indirectly answering through hints. Cattleya discusses the shadow of the Giant King's Court.

682Klein hunts Molsona, a mob boss on Toscarter Island, using Creeping Hunger to kill him. Anderson Hood provides information and accompanies Klein.

683Klein encounters Slaughter Kircheis in a bar and engages in a battle, using his abilities to gain the upper hand.

684Klein continues fighting Kircheis, eventually killing him and grazing his soul with Creeping Hunger, obtaining new Beyonder powers.

685Klein claims the bounty for Kircheis and investigates a blood crystal from his corpse, divining its origins to the Death Announcer.

686Klein uses divination to learn about the Priest of Light's potion formulas and the blood crystal's connection to the Blatherer.

687Klein and Anderson leave Toscarter Island via a liner, with Klein preparing for potential retaliation from King of Immortality.

688Klein meets Councilor Ricciardo to remove Anderson's bad luck curse and learns about the Mother Tree of Desire.

689Anderson's curse is removed, and Klein plans to travel to Bayam for a mystical revolver, while Danitz contacts Klein for help regarding Edwina's disappearance.

690Klein receives Danitz's letter via Reinette Tinekerr, learns about Edwina's vanishing, and decides to investigate using Groselle's Travels.

691Klein, Anderson, and Danitz enter Groselle's Travels by smearing blood on the book, arriving in a snowy world.

692They meet the Golden Dream's crew and Klein examines the book, confirming Edwina is trapped inside.

693Klein explains the situation to Danitz, who agrees to help, and they enter the book world together.

694Klein, Danitz, and Anderson enter Groselle's Travels, with Klein instructing Danitz to recite The Fool's honorific name for assistance.

695They find each other in the snowy landscape and take shelter in a cave, encountering the frost dragon Ulyssan's aura.

696They meet Groselle and his team, including Edwina, and learn about the world's rules and their goal to defeat Ulyssan.

697The group discusses history with Mobet Zoroast, revealing insights about the Fourth Epoch and Kings of Angels.

698Snowman mentions the Angel of Imagination Adam, triggering Ulyssan's attack, and the battle begins.

699The team fights Ulyssan, with Groselle tanking attacks and others using their abilities in coordinated efforts.

700The battle continues, with Klein using invisibility to control Ulyssan's Spirit Body Threads, and the team working together to weaken the dragon.
