# Ch. 1–100 Summaries

Lord of the Mysteries

Transmigration and Mystery

1Zhou Mingrui wakes up as Klein Moretti after a transmigration, discovering a bloody scene, a revolver, and a notebook with the text 'Everyone will die, including me.'

2Klein investigates his situation, cleans up the blood, and recalls the luck enhancement ritual he performed on Earth, planning to try it again to return home.

3Zhou Mingrui interacts with his sister Melissa, learning about their family's financial struggles and planning to attempt the luck enhancement ritual again.

4Zhou Mingrui goes shopping, encounters a tarot divination by an animal trainer, and prepares for the luck enhancement ritual with local translations.

5Zhou Mingrui performs the luck enhancement ritual, which transports him to a gray fog world where he accidentally summons Audrey Hall and Alger Wilson.

6In the Gray Fog World, Klein establishes himself as The Fool, and Audrey and Alger discuss Beyonder potions, with Alger offering the Sailor and Spectator formulas.

7The trio establishes the Tarot Club with designations (The Fool, Justice, The Hanged Man) and agrees to meet weekly. Zhou Mingrui accepts his new identity as Klein.

8Alger returns to his ship and kills a captive, while Audrey attends a ceremony in Backlund showcasing new military technology. Both reflect on the new era begun by The Fool.

9Klein reads Klein's notebook, discovering entries about deciphering the Antigonus family notebook with Welch and Naya, and the ominous final line.

10Police inspectors question Klein about Welch's and Naya's deaths, revealing they died in bizarre suicides. Klein pretends amnesia and is warned he is lucky to be alive.

11Klein cooks a improved mutton stew for Melissa, bonding with her, while remaining anxious about the police investigation.

12Klein has a nightmare about being interrogated by Dunn Smith, then wakes to find Dunn actually outside his door in the middle of the night.

13Dunn Smith reveals he is a Nighthawk who entered Klein's dream, confirms Klein's memory loss, and takes him to meet an expert at Welch's apartment.

14Daly, the Spirit Medium, examines Klein using fragrances and seances but finds no traces of lost memories, clearing him of direct involvement.

15Dunn invites Klein to join the Nighthawks as a civilian staff member, explaining the salary and conditions, and drops him off at home.

16Klein decides to accept Dunn's offer, goes to the Hound Pub for directions, and finds the Nighthawks' front as the Blackthorn Security Company.

17Klein meets Rozanne and enters the Nighthawks' underground facility, meeting Dunn at the Chanis Gate and signing his contract.

18Dunn explains the origins of Beyonders, the potion system, and the five types of losing control, emphasizing the dangers of the path.

19Dunn describes Sealed Artifacts and their grades, showing Klein a photo of Ince Zangwill, who escaped with Sealed Artifact 0-08.

20Klein gets an advance payment from Mrs. Orianna, meets Old Neil who shows him Roselle's diary written in Simplified Chinese, and is assigned reading duties.

21Klein reads Emperor Roselle's diary written in Simplified Chinese, learning key insights about potion digestion and acting, and feels a connection to his fellow transmigrator.

22Rozanne explains the Church's Beyonder sequences to Klein, detailing Sleepless, Midnight Poet, and Nightmare, while Klein considers his options for becoming a Beyonder.

23Klein buys a cane for self-defense and celebrates his new job with Melissa, though she is hesitant about spending money on dining out.

24Klein informs Tingen University about not attending the interview, meets his mentor Cohen and Azik, and later feels being watched, leading him to seek help at Saint Selena Cathedral.

25Klein seeks help from Dunn Smith via the cathedral after being followed, and is advised to act normally while under surveillance to avoid alerting the observer.

26Klein practices shooting at the Zouteland Shooting Club to improve his self-defense skills, then returns home to have dinner with Benson and Melissa.

27An intruder from the Secret Order sneaks into Klein's room, but Dunn Smith intervenes in a dream, and Klein helps capture him by forcing him into the corridor.

28Dunn explains the Secret Order's motives related to the Antigonus notebook and offers Klein a choice to become a Beyonder, presenting options like Mystery Pryer, Corpse Collector, and Seer.

29The Moretti siblings search for a new home with Tingen City Housing Improvement Company and find a terrace house on Daffodil Street after considering their options.

30Klein moves into the new house with Benson and Melissa, and the next day, he tells Dunn Smith his decision to become a Seer, marking a brand new beginning.

31Old Neil concocts the Seer potion, and Klein consumes it, becoming a Beyonder and experiencing initial side effects like enhanced perception and murmurs.

32Old Neil teaches Klein Spirit Vision, explaining the layers of the human spirit and how to use and control the ability to see auras and spiritual bodies.

33Klein practices switching Spirit Vision on and off using a glabella tap, returns home exhausted, and prepares for the Tarot Club gathering in the afternoon.

34In the Tarot Club, Alger provides the Spectator potion formula to Audrey, and Klein shares insights from Roselle's diary about digesting and acting, offering it as an advance payment.

35The Tarot Club members discuss collecting Roselle's secret diary, and Klein suggests a civil service examination system to Audrey and Alger as a solution to government inefficiency.

36After the Tarot Club gathering, Klein reflects on his new abilities and decides to act as a Seer, planning to join the Divination Club for practical experience.

37Klein visits the Divination Club and has his fortune told by Glacis, using the opportunity to practice his perception and Spirit Vision on a novice fortune-teller.

38Leonard Mitchell questions Klein about the Antigonus notebook, raising suspicions about the suicide events, and Klein goes to practice shooting again at the club.

39Klein and Melissa discuss finances, and he convinces her to take public transportation to save time for studies, showing his care for her well-being.

40Old Neil teaches Klein spirit pendulum divination and basic mysticism symbols, and encourages him to join the Divination Club for practice to better act as a Seer.

41Klein works at Blackthorn Security Company reading mysticism materials, while Audrey successfully concocts the Spectator potion but her dog Susie drinks it first, forcing her to make a second batch.

42Butler Klee hires Blackthorn Security Company to find kidnapped Elliott Vickroy, and Leonard Mitchell takes the mission with Klein's help.

43Klein and Leonard use divination to locate Elliott, finding him in a building with three kidnappers.

44Leonard uses his Midnight Poem ability to put the kidnappers to sleep, and they rescue Elliott after a confrontation.

45Klein has a nightmare revealing that the Antigonus family notebook is in the apartment across from the kidnappers, and he informs Dunn Smith.

46Dunn, Klein, and others investigate Ray Bieber's apartment, finding his mother's corpse and using divination to create a portrait of Ray.

47Old Neil explains that the notebook is likely with Ray Bieber, and Klein is now safer from the Secret Order's attention.

48Klein joins the Divination Club, meets Hanass Vincent and Edward Steve, and practices his Seer abilities.

49Hanass teaches magic mirror divination, and Glacis returns, crediting Klein for diagnosing his illness.

50Klein gives his brother Benson five pounds from his commission, and Old Neil demonstrates ritualistic magic to pay off a debt.

51Old Neil performs a ritualistic magic that leads him to find a lost wallet, settling his debt through what he calls 'the power of magic.'

52Audrey attends a banquet as a Spectator, observing people's emotions and details, but is distracted by her dog Susie.

53Alger Wilson sails the seas pursuing a Listener from the Aurora Order, while a boy named Jack is taken by his father to a holy place.

54Klein has his first divination client, Anna, who seeks news of her missing fiancé Joyce Meyer.

55Klein uses dream divination to learn that Joyce has returned home, and Anna confirms this, establishing Klein's reputation as a seer.

56Joyce recounts his ordeal with pirates and mutiny on the Alfalfa, and Anna praises Klein's accurate divination.

57Klein organizes his thoughts on potion digestion, acting, the luck enhancement ritual, and the Antigonus notebook in his notes.

58Klein prepares for the Tarot Club gathering and devises a ritualistic magic method for members to request leave.

59In the Tarot Club, Audrey and Alger provide pages of Roselle's diary, revealing his origins as a Savant and the concept of acting.

60Alger shares information about the Antigonus family and the second Blasphemy Slate, and Klein teaches a modified ritual for communication.

61Audrey and Alger perform the ritualistic magic designed by The Fool, and Klein reflects on the gray fog world's mysteries.

62Klein visits the Divination Club, divines for Glacis about an investment, and meets Joyce Meyer who seeks dream interpretation.

63Klein interprets Joyce Meyer's recurring nightmare, revealing hidden suspicions about Tris from the Alfalfa tragedy.

64Audrey and Alger attempt the ritual; Klein learns about Instigator Tris and the Theosophy Order from Dunn Smith.

65Klein reads Nighthawk documents on Beyonder sequences and secret organizations, gaining knowledge about various pathways.

66Klein learns about the Demoness Sect and Witch potion; Tris receives the Witch formula from a Demoness Sect member.

67Klein responds to Audrey's and Alger's rituals in the gray fog world, confirming the ritual's effectiveness.

68Klein and Old Neil visit the underground market at Evil Dragon Bar, where Tris escapes a police operation.

69Klein buys silver accessories to make amulets and helps a teenage girl choose an amulet at the market.

70Sealed Artifact 2-049 arrives in Tingen; Klein and the Nighthawks use it to track Ray Bieber to a warehouse.

71The Nighthawks encounter the suited clown from the Secret Order while investigating the warehouse with Sealed Artifact 2-049.

72A battle ensues with the suited clown; Sealed Artifact 2-049 is released and begins tracking Ray Bieber.

73Ray Bieber, transformed into a monster, attacks the Nighthawks; Klein uses divination to warn of dangers.

74The Nighthawks defeat Ray Bieber, but Klein is nearly controlled by Sealed Artifact 2-049 after the explosion.

75Klein uses the luck enhancement ritual to escape control, tricks the suited clown into Sealed Artifact 2-049's range, and kills him.

76The Nighthawks deal with the aftermath, retrieve the Antigonus family notebook, and discuss Klein's potential formal membership.

77Klein and the Nighthawks search for remnant items from Ray Bieber; Dunn explains the Clown potion as Seer's advancement.

78Klein helps Frye with the suited clown's corpse, which momentarily revives and murmurs 'Hornacis', causing shock.

79Klein recovers from the trauma, receives a letter from his mentor about Hornacis mountain range, and plans for Selena's birthday.

80Klein learns that Sealed Artifact 2-049 and the notebook are being escorted to Backlund, and Rozanne jokes about him getting married to recruit new staff.

81Klein discusses with Old Neil about becoming an official Nighthawk and the Clown potion, while Bogda seeks divination for his liver disease from Klein.

82Klein divines for Bogda and directs him to Lawson's Folk Herb Store, where the apothecary sells him herbs for his disease.

83Bogda takes the herbs and recovers, while Klein creates protective amulets and is officially promoted to a Nighthawk by Dunn.

84Klein attends Selena's birthday banquet with his siblings, meets Elizabeth, and senses supernatural fluctuations upstairs.

85Klein investigates the supernatural activity, confirms Selena's dangerous magic mirror divination, and enlists Elizabeth's help to lure her into a ritual.

86Klein performs a suspension-style ritual to cleanse Selena of the evil entity, successfully saving her but exhausting his spirituality.

87Klein instructs Elizabeth to keep the incident secret and reports Hanass Vincent to Dunn, who later dies mysteriously.

88Dunn assigns Klein an initiation mission to investigate Sir Deweyville's harassment by supernatural moans.

89Klein, in probationary inspector uniform, meets Inspector Tolle and Sir Deweyville to begin the investigation into the haunting moans.

90Klein uses Spirit Vision and dream divination to discover that the moans are from resentful spirits of lead-poisoned workers, particularly Hayley Walker.

91Klein advises Deweyville to compensate Hayley's parents, which resolves the haunting, and explains it as mass hysteria to the others.

92The Tarot Club gathers; Audrey and Alger provide Roselle's diary pages, and Klein shares insights on potion digestion and acting.

93In the Tarot Club, they discuss the Psychology Alchemists, and Klein reveals information about the Fallen Creator from Roselle's diary.

94Alger shares information about hidden organizations like the Moses Ascetic Order, and Audrey asks about adding new members to the Tarot Club.

95Klein hears a prayer from a teenager in a foreign language above the gray fog, and later meets Bogda at the Divination Club, who praises his recovery.

96Daly visits and discusses Sequence pathways with Dunn, while Klein begins combat training with instructor Gawain.

97Klein trains with Gawain and attends a dinner with the Nighthawks to celebrate his official membership, meeting Seeka Tron and others.

98Klein visits Khoy University, reads about the Hornacis main peak, and Azik warns him about coincidences in his fate using divination.

99Klein investigates the coincidences by divining above the gray fog, discovering that Elliott's kidnapping had extraordinary elements behind it.

100Klein continues divining for clues to the Clown potion, which leads to images related to the Antigonus family notebook, and he prays to himself to draw a portrait of a key person.
